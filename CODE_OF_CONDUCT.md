# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in layerlab a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Acknowledging that other people may have different experience and viewpoints.
- Accepting feedback gracefully and giving it kindly.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication about the project, and also when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respond with actions they consider appropriate, which may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences as determined by the other maintainers.

The maintainer contact for reports is to be published in the repository before the first public release.
